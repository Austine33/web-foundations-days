About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing your thoughts quickly. It helps you write, organise and find your notes without any fuss.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and click "New note".
  2. Type your title and your note.
  3. Save your note and find it later in your list.

Features

  • Fast note creation
  • Simple, clean layout
  • Easy searching

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save the note
